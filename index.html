Settings

On iPhone, Safari does not normally offer programmable vibration. Sound cues and running timers may be suspended when you lock the phone or switch apps. Keep this app visible to receive cues.

Using alongside Strava
1. Start GPS recording in Strava at the traffic lights.
2. Jog to Kenilworth Avenue, complete dynamic mobility and two gradual accelerations.
3. Switch to this coach and select Start; it automatically alternates efforts and walking recoveries.
4. Pause freezes the countdown. During recovery, Ready now starts the next uphill rep immediately.
5. After the final rep, jog back to the lights and save your Strava activity.

Use a clear uphill section away from junctions and driveways. Stop for knee pain, swelling or instability; avoid all-out sprinting after a recent break.
